On this page we are going to be reviewing food!

First off, Chicken Valley. While some might say that this is nothing but a mere ripoff of Chicken Palace, i believe this is much more than that. The food from here is delectable, especiallyy their chicken filet burgers. They have the perfect baalnce between cheese, mayo, chicken and letttuce. The only downside is the Sesame seeds... or the lack of them. In my opinion, if the bun had Sesame seeds, it would be infinitely better.
